Santa's Secret Draw

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

In short: Santa's Secret Draw does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

To run a draw, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Group names, the names of people in each group, and their optional card codes.
  • Rules about who cannot pick whom.
  • The current draw: who gives to whom, and who has already seen their pick.
  • Your app preferences, such as how picks are shown.
  • The organizer PIN, which is kept in the iPhone Keychain.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no network connections and has no servers.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Families and groups that include children may use the app. Because the app collects nothing, no information about children, or anyone else, is collected or shared. Names entered for a draw stay on the device where they were typed.

Deleting your data

You can delete any group in the app by swiping left on it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored from your iPhone.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, the app's saved data may be included in that backup, as with most apps. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, analytics or tracking to it. GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
