Santa's Secret Draw

Santa's Secret Draw

Pass the phone. Keep the secret.

The Secret Santa draw for one shared iPhone. Everyone taps their name, draws, and sees who they picked, right there in the room. Nobody picks themselves, and your rules always hold.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

Set it up once, then let the phone do the rest.

1

Add your people

Make a group for the family, the team or the office. Add card codes if you are using labeled envelopes.

2

Set the rules

Keep partners, siblings or last year's pairs apart, one way or both ways. Then start the draw.

3

Pass the phone

Each person taps their name, presses Draw, and sees their pick. Names lock as people draw.

A little holiday magic

Every pick is revealed on a gift tag, one person at a time.

Made for the room

Everything an organizer needs, and nothing they don't.

Fair every time

Every pick is decided at once, so the last person is never stuck with themselves.

Who can't pick whom

Exclusion rules one way or both ways. The app tells you if a draw is impossible.

Names or card codes

Show each pick as a name, or as a code like H85 that matches a labeled envelope.

Organizer PIN

Settings and the full list stay behind a PIN, away from curious guests.

Easy redraws

Reset a draw and the next one avoids repeating the same pairs when it can.

Works offline

No sign-up, no internet needed. Perfect for a living room, a locker room or a party.

Your secrets stay secret

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
