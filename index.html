Santa's Secret Draw

Home Privacy Support

A Secret Santa draw for one shared iPhone. Pass the phone around the room: each person taps their name, presses Draw, and sees who they picked, as a name or a short card code. Nobody ever picks themselves, and the rules you set are always respected.

  • Save groups for family, friends, teams and the office.
  • Set rules for who cannot pick whom, one way or both ways.
  • Show each pick as a name, or as a card code that matches a labeled envelope.
  • Organizer settings are protected by a PIN.
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
